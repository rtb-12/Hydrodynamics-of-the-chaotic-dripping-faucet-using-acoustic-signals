What changes with height

A drop rings when it traps a small air bubble that keeps sounding a clear tone for a few milliseconds; without one it only clicks. In the plots each dot is one drop, spread a little sideways around its height so dots do not hide each other, and a bar marks the median. Faint dots are drops that did not ring. ρ is the rank correlation with height: near ±1 is a steady trend, near 0 none.
