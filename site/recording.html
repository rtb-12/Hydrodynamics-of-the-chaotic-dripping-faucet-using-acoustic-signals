Recordings

Each recording pairs a video of the drip with sound from a separate phone microphone, lined up to one clock. Drops are counted from the video alone, so the count checks what the microphone heard. The video and sound load fully before playback, so seeking and slow motion stay smooth. Click any graph to enlarge it, and use the i buttons to see how each quantity is measured.
